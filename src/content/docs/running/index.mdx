Once your stack is up, most of what you do falls into a handful of shapes:
starting the part you need, checking on something that was asked for, adjusting
quality, and keeping a backup you could actually restore from.

These pages cover each of those. None of them assume you have read the others.

## Where to go next

If you have not set anything up yet, [Start here](/start/) comes first —
particularly [Your first stack](/start/your-first-stack/).

When something is wrong, [When something's wrong](/fixing/) is the section to
open. [Run the doctor](/fixing/run-the-doctor/) is almost always the right first
move, because it checks the claims rather than repeating them.

For the exact flags on any command, see [Commands](/commands/), and for the
terminal dashboard, [The TUI](/commands/the-tui/).

lemonfiber runs a media stack for you — the indexers, the download clients, the
automation services and the library — and does the parts that are usually manual:
guided setup, wiring the services to each other, and checking that what it built
is actually doing what you asked.

These four pages take you from nothing to a running stack. Read them in order the
first time; afterwards, [Running your stack](/running/) is where you will spend
your time.

## Where to go next

Once your stack is up, [Running your stack](/running/) covers the day-to-day:
which slice to start, what each service is for, quality, requests from the
household, and where a particular show got to.

When something is not working, go straight to
[When something's wrong](/fixing/) — the doctor is the first thing to reach for,
not the last.

If you would rather read the requirements this is all built against, they live in
[the specification](/spec/).
